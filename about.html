About Dr. Chethan Kumar

Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon in JP Nagar and RR Nagar, Bengaluru. Patient-first, evidence-based orthopaedic care.

Personal Brand

Fellowship-trained. Compassionate. Focused on mobility.

Dr. Chethan Kumar is a consultant orthopaedic surgeon serving patients in JP Nagar, RR Nagar, Banashankari, Uttarahalli, Kanakapura Road, Jayanagar and South Bengaluru.

He combines surgical expertise with conservative treatment approaches, helping patients understand their condition and explore all appropriate options before making a decision.

Qualifications MBBS, MS in Orthopaedics, Fellowship in Joint Replacement and Sports Medicine.

Hospital Affiliations: Operating privileges and visiting hospitals will be added after confirmation by the clinic.

Treatment Philosophy: Treat the person, not just the MRI. The goal is to restore function and mobility through the least invasive, effective treatment plan appropriate for each patient.

Areas of Expertise

Hip and Knee Replacement

Primary joint replacement for advanced arthritis, supported by structured rehabilitation.

Arthroscopy

Minimally invasive procedures for conditions affecting the meniscus, ACL, shoulder and cartilage.

Sports Injuries

Assessment and treatment of ligament, tendon and overuse injuries in amateur and competitive athletes.

Trauma and Fractures

Care for accident- and fall-related bone injuries, ranging from simple to complex fractures.

Spine and Shoulder Conditions

Evaluation and treatment of slipped discs, sciatica, frozen shoulder, rotator cuff problems and cervical spondylosis.

Bone Health

Osteoporosis counselling and fracture-risk reduction for older adults.
