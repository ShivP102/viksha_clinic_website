About Dr. Chethan Kumar

Consultant Orthopedic Surgeon — JP Nagar & RR Nagar, Bengaluru. Patient-first, evidence-based orthopedic care.

Personal branding

Fellowship-trained. Compassionate. Focused on mobility.

Dr. Chethan Kumar is a consultant orthopedic surgeon serving JP Nagar, RR Nagar, Banashankari, Uttarahalli, Kanakapura Road, Jayanagar and Bengaluru South. He combines surgical expertise with conservative care so patients understand every option before they decide.

Qualifications (to be confirmed by the clinic): MBBS, MS (Orthopedics), Fellowship in Joint Replacement & Sports Medicine.

Hospital affiliations (placeholder): Operating privileges and visiting hospitals will be listed here after the clinic confirms them.

Philosophy: Treat the person, not only the MRI. Restore function with the least invasive effective plan.

Areas of expertise

Hip & knee replacement

Primary replacement for advanced arthritis, with structured rehabilitation.

Arthroscopy

Keyhole procedures for meniscus, ACL, shoulder and cartilage problems.

Sports injuries

Ligament, tendon and overuse injuries in amateur and competitive athletes.

Trauma & fractures

Accident and fall-related bone injuries, from simple to complex.

Spine & shoulder

Slip disc, sciatica, frozen shoulder, rotator cuff and cervical spondylosis.

Bone health

Osteoporosis counselling and fracture-risk reduction for older adults.
