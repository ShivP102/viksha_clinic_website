Best Exercises for Back Pain

Patient education from Dr. Chethan Kumar’s orthopedic practice in JP Nagar and RR Nagar, Bengaluru.

Exercise is among the most effective treatments for common mechanical back pain. The “best” exercises are the ones you can do without sharp leg pain, and that you will repeat. There is no single miracle stretch for every slip disc.

In the first days of a flare, short walks and comfortable positions beat complete bed rest. Gentle pelvic tilts, knee-to-chest stretches (if they ease symptoms), and diaphragmatic breathing reduce spasm.

As pain settles, add core endurance: bird-dog, side plank regressions, and glute bridges. Walking in JP Nagar or Banashankari parks is useful if sitting at a desk all day is your trigger.

Sciatica that worsens with forward bending may prefer extension-based movements; pain that worsens with standing may prefer flexion. A physiotherapist should individualize this. Stop any exercise that causes progressive numbness or weakness.

Red flags — saddle anaesthesia, bladder or bowel change, fever, unexplained weight loss, or trauma — are not “exercise problems.” They need urgent medical care.

Combine movement with sleep, stress management and workstation setup. Neck-to-low-back ergonomics matter for cervical and lumbar discs alike.

This is general education, not a personal exercise prescription. If you have a known large disc prolapse, get clearance before a new gym programme.
