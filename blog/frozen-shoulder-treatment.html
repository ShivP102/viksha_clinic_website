Frozen Shoulder Treatment

Patient education from Dr. Chethan Kumar’s orthopedic practice in JP Nagar and RR Nagar, Bengaluru.

Frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis) makes the shoulder stiff in all directions. It is more common in people with diabetes and can last many months even with good treatment. That timeline is frustrating but important to understand.

Stages often described: painful freezing, stiff frozen, and thawing. Night pain is typical early. Later, the main problem is reaching behind the back or overhead.

Treatment: education, analgesia, and physiotherapy that respects pain. Aggressive stretching that causes severe flares can set you back. Injections (steroid, sometimes hydrodilatation) help selected patients.

Surgery (manipulation or arthroscopic release) is reserved for stiffness that remains disabling after adequate conservative care. Most patients in JP Nagar and Banashankari never need it.

Rule out rotator cuff tear, arthritis and referred neck pain — they can mimic or coexist with frozen shoulder. Examination is more useful than jumping to MRI in typical cases, though imaging is used when the story is atypical.

Diabetes control, if relevant, should be optimized. Recovery is measured in months. Book a review if you cannot tuck your shirt or sleep despite a structured programme.
