Hip Replacement Recovery Guide

Patient education from Dr. Chethan Kumar’s orthopedic practice in JP Nagar and RR Nagar, Bengaluru.

Recovery after hip replacement is a process, not a date. This guide outlines typical phases; your surgeon’s protocol always wins if it differs.

Hospital: pain control, breathing exercises, and walking with a walker often begin within a day. Blood clot prevention (stockings, medicines, movement) is standard.

Weeks 1–6: wound care, sleeping positions as advised, and gradual increase in walking indoors then outdoors. Many patients from RR Nagar and Uttarahalli manage stairs with a rail and a defined technique.

Precautions (avoiding certain bends or crosses) depend on surgical approach. Do not copy a neighbour’s rules.

Weeks 6–12: physiotherapy shifts from protection to strength and walking quality. Driving is cleared only when you can safely control the vehicle and are off impairing medicines — ask your surgeon.

By 3–6 months many people walk community distances comfortably. High-impact sport is usually discouraged long term; swimming and walking are friendlier to the implant.

Call the clinic for fever, wound discharge, sudden calf pain, or a new inability to bear weight. This article cannot cover every complication.
