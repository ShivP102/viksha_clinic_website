When Do You Need Knee Replacement?

Patient education from Dr. Chethan Kumar’s orthopedic practice in JP Nagar and RR Nagar, Bengaluru.

Knee replacement is a quality-of-life operation. You “need” it when the daily cost of an arthritic knee — pain, stiffness, lost independence — outweighs the recovery of surgery, and X-rays confirm end-stage joint damage.

Typical candidates have bone-on-bone arthritis, pain that limits walking or sleep, and have already tried physiotherapy, weight management, medicines and sometimes injections. Patients from Banashankari and Kanakapura Road often delay because they fear surgery; delay is reasonable until function is clearly declining.

You may not need replacement yet if pain is mild, walking is still comfortable with simple measures, or X-rays show only early narrowing. Arthroscopy rarely “cures” advanced arthritis.

Questions your surgeon should answer: partial versus total replacement, implant choice in broad terms, anaesthesia, hospital stay, blood clots prevention, and realistic walking goals. No ethical surgeon guarantees a perfect knee.

Medical optimization (diabetes, blood pressure, dental infections, smoking) reduces complications. Family support for the first two weeks at home matters as much as the operation.

If you are comparing “best orthopedic surgeon in JP Nagar” listings, look for a doctor who explains non-surgical options honestly and does not rush you.
