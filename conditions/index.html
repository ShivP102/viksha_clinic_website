Conditions treated

Common orthopedic problems seen in clinic. Tap a card for a short overview.

Knee Pain

Knee Pain

Often from osteoarthritis, meniscus injury or overuse. Exam and X-rays guide whether rest, physio or surgery is next.

Hip Pain

Tap for details

Hip Pain

Groin pain that limits walking may be hip arthritis or AVN. Conservative care first; replacement when the joint is worn.

Related care →

Shoulder Pain

Tap for details

Shoulder Pain

Overhead pain and night pain are common. Rotator cuff problems and impingement are assessed before considering keyhole surgery.

Related care →

Frozen Shoulder

Tap for details

Frozen Shoulder

Stiffness in all directions, more common with diabetes. Physiotherapy is first-line; injections help selected patients.

Related care →

Tennis Elbow

Tap for details

Tennis Elbow

Lateral elbow pain from tendon overload — not only tennis. Activity modification and physiotherapy usually suffice.

Related care →

Heel Pain

Tap for details

Heel Pain

First-step morning heel pain often points to plantar fasciitis. Stretching, footwear and load changes are the usual start.

Related care →

Plantar Fasciitis

Tap for details

Plantar Fasciitis

Inflammation of the plantar fascia. Calf stretching, supportive footwear and load management are core treatments.

Related care →

Ankle Ligament Injury

Tap for details

Ankle Ligament Injury

Twisting injuries can stretch the ATFL. Bracing and rehab help prevent chronic instability.

Related care →

Wrist Pain

Tap for details

Wrist Pain

Falls, desk work and TFCC injuries can cause wrist pain. Persistent swelling should be checked with an X-ray.

Related care →

Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

Tap for details

Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

Night tingling in the thumb, index and middle fingers. Splints and activity change first; surgery if compression is severe.

Related care →

Osteoarthritis

Tap for details

Osteoarthritis

Wear of joint cartilage, especially in knees and hips. Weight, exercise and medicines first; replacement for advanced disease.

Related care →

Rheumatoid Arthritis

Tap for details

Rheumatoid Arthritis

Inflammatory arthritis, often in several joints. Orthopedic care works with your rheumatologist for deformity and joint damage.

Related care →

Sciatica

Tap for details

Sciatica

Leg pain from lumbar nerve irritation, often linked to a slip disc. Most cases start with guided conservative care.

Related care →

Cervical Radiculopathy

Tap for details

Cervical Radiculopathy

Arm pain from a pinched nerve in the neck. Exam and imaging decide between physio, medicines or further care.

Related care →

Disc Prolapse

Tap for details

Disc Prolapse

A herniated disc may compress a nerve. Most improve without surgery unless there are red-flag symptoms.

Related care →

Bone Fractures

Tap for details

Bone Fractures

Broken bones from falls or accidents. Some heal in plaster; others need fixation when alignment or stability is lost.

Related care →

Ligament Injuries

Tap for details

Ligament Injuries

ACL, ankle and other ligaments. A pop and swelling after sport needs prompt assessment before return to play.
