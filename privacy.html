Privacy policy

This website collects only the information you type into the appointment form: name, phone, optional email, preferred clinic, service, date and message. That information is sent by you through WhatsApp to the clinic number listed in site configuration.

We do not sell personal data. Form details are used only to schedule and follow up on orthopaedic consultations. Do not send sensitive medical records over WhatsApp unless you are comfortable doing so; you may bring reports to the clinic instead.

This site may use basic analytics in the future. Placeholder clinic contact details will be replaced with official numbers when provided.

For privacy questions, email the address shown in the footer once real contact details are published.
