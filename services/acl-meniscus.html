ACL / Meniscus Injury Treatment

Twisting injuries on the football or cricket field often involve the ACL or meniscus. Care is planned around your sport, age and knee stability.

Related conditions

Knee pain · Ligament injuries

Symptoms

  • A pop at injury, swelling within hours
  • Giving way on turning
  • Locking if a meniscus fragment catches
  • Difficulty pivoting

Causes

  • Non-contact plant-and-turn
  • Contact tackle
  • Degenerative meniscus in older adults

Diagnosis

  • Lachman and pivot-shift tests
  • McMurray test for meniscus
  • MRI confirmation

Treatment options

  • Early physiotherapy for swelling and range
  • ACL reconstruction for unstable knees in active patients
  • Meniscus repair when the tear pattern allows

Recovery timeline

ACL rehab is typically 6–9+ months before unrestricted pivoting sport. Meniscus repair may have weight-bearing restrictions.

FAQs

Partial injuries or low-demand patients may cope with rehab and bracing. Complete tears in pivoting athletes often need reconstruction.
Repair is preferred for suitable tears to protect cartilage.
