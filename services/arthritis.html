Arthritis Treatment

A joint pain specialist in Bengaluru South for osteoarthritis and coordination of rheumatoid arthritis orthopedic care alongside your physician.

Symptoms

  • Morning stiffness
  • Activity-related joint pain
  • Swelling and reduced range
  • Multiple joints in inflammatory disease

Causes

  • Osteoarthritis wear
  • Rheumatoid and other inflammatory arthritis
  • Post-traumatic arthritis

Diagnosis

  • Pattern of joints involved
  • Blood tests when inflammation is suspected
  • X-rays; MRI selectively

Treatment options

  • Education, exercise, weight management
  • Medicines and intra-articular injections
  • Joint replacement for end-stage osteoarthritis

Recovery timeline

Arthritis is managed long term. Flares settle; joint replacement recovery follows procedure-specific protocols.

FAQs

Osteoarthritis is more common with age, but inflammatory arthritis can start younger.
No diet cures osteoarthritis. Healthy weight helps knees and hips.
