Arthroscopy (Keyhole Joint Surgery)

Arthroscopy uses a camera and small incisions to treat meniscus tears, loose bodies, selected ligament and shoulder problems with typically faster recovery than open surgery.

Symptoms

  • Clicking, locking or giving way of the knee
  • Shoulder catching or persistent pain after injury
  • Swelling that does not settle

Causes

  • Sports twisting injuries
  • Degenerative meniscus tears
  • Shoulder impingement or labral issues

Diagnosis

  • Examination tests
  • X-ray to rule out arthritis or fracture
  • MRI for soft-tissue detail

Treatment options

  • Physiotherapy first when appropriate
  • Arthroscopic meniscus repair or trimming
  • Shoulder arthroscopy for selected rotator cuff and impingement cases

Recovery timeline

Day-care or short stay is common. Return to desk work may be days; return to sport is staged.
