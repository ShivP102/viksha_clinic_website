Back Pain & Slip Disc Treatment

Most back pain and many disc prolapses improve without surgery. Assessment in JP Nagar or RR Nagar helps rule out red flags and start the right physio.

Related conditions

Sciatica · Disc prolapse

Symptoms

  • Low back pain with or without leg radiation (sciatica)
  • Numbness or tingling in a dermatome
  • Worse with sitting or coughing
  • Red flags: saddle numbness, bladder issues — emergency

Causes

  • Disc prolapse
  • Muscle strain
  • Degenerative disc disease
  • Poor ergonomics

Diagnosis

  • Neurological examination
  • X-ray
  • MRI when sciatica persists or red flags exist

Treatment options

  • Relative rest, medicines, physiotherapy
  • Epidural or nerve-root injections in selected cases
  • Surgery for persistent nerve compression or emergency cauda equina

Recovery timeline

Acute episodes often settle in weeks. Core strengthening reduces recurrence. Post-surgical timelines are individualized.
