Fracture & Trauma Surgery

As a trauma surgeon serving RR Nagar and South Bengaluru, Dr. Chethan Kumar treats fractures from simple undisplaced breaks to injuries needing surgical fixation.

Symptoms

  • Pain, swelling, deformity after injury
  • Inability to bear weight
  • Open wounds over a fracture (emergency)
  • Numbness — seek urgent care

Causes

  • Road traffic accidents
  • Falls at home, especially in older adults
  • Sports collisions

Diagnosis

  • Examination including nerve and circulation checks
  • X-rays in two planes
  • CT for complex joint fractures

Treatment options

  • Plaster or brace for stable fractures
  • Surgical fixation (plates, nails, screws) when alignment or stability is poor
  • Open fractures need emergency hospital care

Recovery timeline

Bone healing often takes 6–12 weeks; joints and muscles need longer rehab. Follow-up X-rays guide when you can load the limb.

FAQs
