Hip Replacement in RR Nagar & Bengaluru South

Hip replacement restores comfort for patients with advanced hip arthritis or avascular necrosis, including those travelling from Kanakapura Road and Uttarahalli.

Related conditions

Hip pain · Osteoarthritis

Symptoms

  • Groin or buttock pain
  • Pain on putting on socks
  • Limping and reduced walking distance
  • Night pain

Causes

  • Osteoarthritis
  • Avascular necrosis
  • Inflammatory arthritis
  • Old trauma

Diagnosis

  • Examination of hip motion and gait
  • X-rays of the pelvis and hip
  • MRI when AVN is suspected

Treatment options

  • Physiotherapy, walking aids, medicines
  • Injections in selected cases
  • Total hip replacement when joint is destroyed

Recovery timeline

Most patients walk with support the day after surgery. Precautions and physio are personalized. Full recovery is gradual over months.
