Knee Replacement in JP Nagar, Bengaluru

Total and partial knee replacement for patients from JP Nagar, Banashankari and Jayanagar whose arthritis no longer responds to medicines and physiotherapy.

Symptoms

  • Pain that limits walking, stairs or sleep
  • Bow-leg or knock-knee deformity
  • Stiffness after sitting
  • Swelling after activity

Causes

  • Osteoarthritis
  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Post-traumatic arthritis after old fractures
  • Avascular necrosis of the knee (less common)

Diagnosis

  • Clinical examination of alignment, range and ligaments
  • Weight-bearing X-rays
  • Occasionally MRI if meniscus or other issues coexist

Treatment options

  • Activity modification, physiotherapy, medicines, injections
  • Partial replacement when only one compartment is worn
  • Total knee replacement for advanced tricompartmental disease

Recovery timeline

Hospital stay is often a few days. Walking with a walker usually starts early. Independent walking and driving timelines vary; physiotherapy continues for weeks to months.

FAQs

When pain and stiffness severely limit life despite non-surgical care, and X-rays show bone-on-bone arthritis.
Many patients improve with weight management, physio and injections. Surgery is elective except in rare destructive disease.
