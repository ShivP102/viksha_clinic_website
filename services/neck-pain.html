Neck Pain & Cervical Spondylosis

Desk work across Bengaluru South makes cervical spondylosis and neck pain common. Care starts with posture, physio and ruling out nerve compression.

Symptoms

  • Neck stiffness
  • Pain radiating to the arm (cervical radiculopathy)
  • Headaches from the neck
  • Hand tingling

Causes

  • Age-related disc and joint wear
  • Forward-head posture
  • Sudden strain

Diagnosis

  • Spurling test and neurological exam
  • X-ray
  • MRI for persistent radiculopathy

Treatment options

  • Physiotherapy, ergonomic advice
  • Medicines for nerve pain when needed
  • Surgery rarely, for progressive weakness or intractable compression

Recovery timeline

Most mechanical neck pain improves in weeks. Nerve symptoms may take longer.
