Osteoporosis & Bone Health

Weak bones raise fracture risk after simple falls — especially in older adults in Bengaluru South. Assessment and medical treatment reduce future fractures.

Symptoms

  • Often silent until a fracture
  • Loss of height
  • Back pain after vertebral compression
  • Hip or wrist fracture from a minor fall

Causes

  • Ageing, menopause
  • Low vitamin D or calcium intake
  • Steroids and some medical conditions
  • Sedentary lifestyle

Diagnosis

  • DEXA bone density when indicated
  • X-rays of fractures
  • Blood tests for secondary causes

Treatment options

  • Falls prevention and exercise
  • Calcium, vitamin D as advised
  • Prescription bone-strengthening medicines when indicated
  • Surgical care of fragility fractures

Recovery timeline

Bone medicines work over months. Rehab after hip or spine fracture is a major part of recovery.
