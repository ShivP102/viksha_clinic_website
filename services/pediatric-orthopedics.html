Pediatric Orthopaedics

Children's bones heal differently. Fractures, limp and growth-related pain in kids from JP Nagar and RR Nagar families are assessed with age-appropriate care.

Related conditions

Bone fractures · Ligament injuries

Symptoms

  • Refusal to use a limb after a fall
  • Limp
  • Night pain that wakes the child
  • Deformity — seek prompt care

Causes

  • Playground and sports injuries
  • Growth plate fractures
  • Developmental conditions (selected cases)

Diagnosis

  • Gentle examination
  • Age-specific X-rays
  • Referral for complex congenital deformity

Treatment options

  • Casting for many pediatric fractures
  • Reduction under anaesthesia when needed
  • Shared care with pediatric orthopaedic centres for complex congenital work

Recovery timeline

Children often heal faster than adults, but growth plates must be protected and followed.

FAQs
