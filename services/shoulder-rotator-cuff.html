Shoulder Pain & Rotator Cuff Care

Shoulder pain from rotator cuff tendinopathy, tears and frozen shoulder is common in desk workers and overhead athletes across Jayanagar and Banashankari.

Symptoms

  • Night pain when lying on the shoulder
  • Weakness on lifting the arm
  • Stiffness in all directions (frozen shoulder)
  • Painful arc during overhead activity

Causes

  • Age-related tendon wear
  • Falls on an outstretched hand
  • Diabetes-related frozen shoulder
  • Repetitive overhead sport or work

Diagnosis

  • Special tests for impingement and cuff strength
  • X-ray
  • Ultrasound or MRI

Treatment options

  • Physiotherapy and activity modification
  • Injections when inflammation dominates
  • Repair of selected full-thickness rotator cuff tears

Recovery timeline

Frozen shoulder often improves over months. Post-repair rehab is longer and must be supervised.
