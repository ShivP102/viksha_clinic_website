Sports Injury Treatment in JP Nagar

A sports injury doctor in JP Nagar helping cricketers, runners and gym-goers from Bengaluru South recover safely — from sprain to surgery when needed.

Related conditions

Ligament injuries · Ankle ligament injury

Symptoms

  • Sudden pop or giving way
  • Swelling within hours of injury
  • Pain that returns with sport
  • Reduced sprinting or jumping

Causes

  • Twisting on the field
  • Overuse and poor load management
  • Inadequate warm-up or muscle imbalance

Diagnosis

  • Sideline history and examination
  • X-ray if fracture suspected
  • MRI for ligaments and cartilage

Treatment options

  • RICE/PEACE & LOVE principles as advised
  • Bracing and physiotherapy
  • Surgical reconstruction for unstable ligaments

Recovery timeline

Return-to-play is criterion-based, not date-based. Premature return raises re-injury risk.
